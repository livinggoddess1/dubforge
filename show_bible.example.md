# Show bible (example — replace with your show)

The adapter reads this before every batch. A short, specific bible improves character voice and jokes more than any model upgrade.

## Tone
Shōnen action-comedy. Big emotional swings: sincere tears one second, slapstick the next. Comedy beats are fast and loud; dramatic beats are quiet.

## Characters
- **Tanjiro** — earnest, polite, kind even to enemies. Speaks simply. Never sarcastic. Calls elders "-san".
- **Zenitsu** — cowardly, melodramatic, whiny; screams and over-reacts constantly. Long panicked rants. Calls Tanjiro by name, Nezuko "Nezuko-chan" (lovestruck).
- **Inosuke** — feral, arrogant, shouts everything, gets names wrong on purpose ("Monitsu", "Gonpachiro"). Keep the wrong names; that's the joke.

## Running jokes / terms
- Breathing styles: "Water Breathing, First Form: Water Surface Slash". Keep official technique names.
- "Demon Slayer Corps", "Hashira".

## Style notes
- Keep it PG-13; don't add swearing the original doesn't have.
- Zenitsu's rants can run long — preserve the energy, trim the words.
